Why I develop

I fell in love with development the moment I realised that code is really just a different kind of design — one where the constraints are logic and the medium is interaction. Building something that actually works, that someone can touch and use, gives me a satisfaction no static mockup ever could.

🎨

Me as a designer

I approach design with deep empathy for the person on the other end of the screen. Good design is mostly invisible — it gets out of the way and lets people do what they came to do. My aesthetic leans minimal and considered, with moments of personality that make an interface feel human.

⚡

Why I love this industry

Technology moves fast enough that there is always something new to learn, always a problem nobody has solved in exactly the right way yet. The intersection of design and development is a rare space where both the analytical and the creative parts of my brain get to work together.
